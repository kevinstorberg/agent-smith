## Conciseness

Every sentence in the ticket earns its place. No filler, no redundancy.

1. Each sentence contributes necessary information — no filler phrases or restating the obvious
2. Sections are proportional to their importance — simple tasks do not have bloated Context or Tasks sections
3. The ticket does not repeat the same information across multiple sections (e.g., Story and Objective saying the same thing)
4. Tasks that always happen together are combined rather than listed separately
