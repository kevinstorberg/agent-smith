---
name: iterate
description: Systematically work through a numbered list of items one at a time, aligning on a solution before acting on each one. Use when asked to "go through these one by one", "work through this list", "fix each of these", or any time a list of issues/improvements/comments needs sequential attention.
---

1. **Catalogue first**: Enumerate every item and present them as a complete numbered list
   before touching any of them. Do not start acting until the full list is visible.

2. **For each item, in order:**
   a. **Describe**: Explain the item in detail — what it is, why it matters, and its impact.
   b. **Propose**: Offer 2–3 concrete solution options with their trade-offs.
   c. **Align**: Stop and wait for the user to select an approach or redirect before writing
      any code.
   d. **Implement**: Apply the agreed solution carefully and completely.
   e. **Confirm**: Briefly state what changed, then explicitly announce the move to the next
      item (e.g. "Item 1 done — moving to item 2").

3. **Close**: Once every item has been addressed, provide a brief summary of all changes made
   and flag any new items discovered during implementation that warrant another pass.
